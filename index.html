How hard to cross

How to play. Roads, buildings and open ground break Singacity's habitats into islands. Animals that can't move between them can't find mates, food or new homes, and small isolated populations slowly disappear. Glowing yellow trails show where each species is trying to travel: a faint wash for quiet trails, strong gold for busy ones, and solid outlined squares for bottlenecks, where one bad development could cut them off. Build crossings, plant to stitch habitat back together, and get every species to its target line.

For ecologists. Each species' landscape is solved with circuit theory (McRae et al. 2008) between home habitats (pairwise, with the weakest pair scored where there are three). Trails are current density as a share of total current, animals are random walkers following that current, and the connection meter is the square root of reference effective resistance over effective resistance now, where the reference is the same landscape with no roads. Resistance values are illustrative.
