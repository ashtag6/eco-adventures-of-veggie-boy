How hard to cross

How to play. Roads, buildings and open ground break Singacity's forests into islands. Animals that can't move between them can't find mates, food or new homes, and small isolated populations slowly disappear. Glowing trails show where each species is most likely to travel, and the brightest spots are bottlenecks where one bad development could cut them off. Build crossings, plant saplings to stitch habitat back together, and reach the connection target for every species. Weather matters too: haze grounds the seed-carrying birds, the monsoon floods underpasses, and thunderstorms flatten exposed planting.

For ecologists. Behind the scenes each species' landscape is solved with circuit theory (McRae et al. 2008). Trails are current density between the two home habitats, the animals are random walkers following that current, and the connection meter is pre-expressway effective resistance divided by effective resistance now.
